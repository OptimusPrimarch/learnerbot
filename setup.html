Set up your own LearnerBot

About ten minutes, once. You get your own private copy: your goals live in your Google Sheet, and the AI uses your free Groq key. Nobody else can see your data, and nothing is shared with the person who sent you here.

1. Make your copy of the sheet

  1. Open the LearnerBot template. Google shows a Make a copy box.
  2. Click Make a copy. The copy goes into your own Google Drive and also includes the small script that powers the sheet.
  3. Use your copy from now on. Please don't type your key or any goals into the template itself.

If Google says you don't have access, ask the person who sent you here to check the template's sharing is set to Anyone with the link can view.

2. Get a free Groq API key

  1. Sign up at console.groq.com/keys and create a key. It starts with gsk_.

3. Save the key in your copy

  1. In your copy of the sheet, wait a few seconds for a LearnerBot menu to appear in the menu bar (reload the page if it doesn't).
  2. Choose LearnerBot → 1. Set Groq API key… and paste the key.
  3. Google will ask you to authorize the script the first time. You may see "Google hasn't verified this app." That is expected, because the script is your own copy. Choose Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) and allow it.

The key is stored in the script's private properties. It is not written into the sheet, and it is never sent to this website.

4. Publish your copy as a web app

  1. In the sheet, choose Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Click Deploy → New deployment, then the gear icon and Web app.
  3. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then Deploy.
  4. Copy the Web app URL it shows (it ends in /exec). You can find it again any time under Deploy → Manage deployments.

"Anyone" only means the URL is reachable. Every request must also carry your secret access token (next step), or it is refused.

5. Connect this website to your sheet

  1. Back in the sheet, choose LearnerBot → 2. Show connection info and copy the Access token. (If a Copy button does nothing, click the box and press Ctrl+C.)
  2. Open LearnerBot, click the ⚙ button, paste the Web app URL from step 4 and the token, and press Save & test.

The URL and the token must come from the same copy of the sheet. If the site says it can't reach your backend, the URL is almost always the problem: copy it again from Deploy → Manage deployments.

The URL and token are saved in this browser only. On another device or browser, paste them again.

On your phone

Once your computer is connected, you don't need to copy anything onto your phone:

  1. On your computer, open ⚙ → Add your phone and press Create a one-time link.
  2. Press Email me the link (it goes only to your own Google address) or Copy link and send it to yourself.
  3. Open the link on your phone. It connects by itself. The link works once and expires after ten minutes.

Your phone gets its own access, separate from your computer's. To cut a phone off (a lost phone, say), open ⚙ → Add your phone on your computer and press Disconnect next to it. LearnerBot → Reset access token in the sheet disconnects every phone at once.

iPhone note: an app added to the Home Screen keeps its own storage, separate from Safari. So add LearnerBot to the Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen), then open the app, tap ⚙ → On a phone? Paste a pairing link, and paste the link there. On Android, install it from the browser menu (Install app or Add to Home screen) after connecting.

Optional: email updates

In LearnerBot, open ⚙ → Email updates and choose what you want and when: a daily focus, a weekly review on the day you pick, and an optional evening nudge that only sends if you haven't logged anything that day. Press Save email settings, and use Send me a test email to check it reaches you.

  • Nothing is sent unless you switch it on. Everything starts off.
  • Emails go only to your own Google account, and only the scheduled ones send. Pressing Run daily in the app never emails you.
  • Times are in your browser's time zone and land within about 15 minutes of the hour you pick (Google doesn't allow more exact).
  • To stop everything at once, use LearnerBot → Turn off all emails in the sheet.

Good to know

  • Keep the token private. Anyone with both the URL and the token can read and change your goals. If it leaks, use LearnerBot → Reset access token, then paste the new one into ⚙.
  • Speed. Each action is a round trip to Google, usually one to three seconds. AI actions (daily, weekly, chat) take 10–20 seconds.
  • Limits. AI requests are capped at 40 an hour per copy to protect your Groq quota.
  • Updates. The website updates for everyone automatically. Your copy of the sheet script only changes if you re-copy the template, so you may be asked to do that occasionally.
